How to open Glimpse
===================

Glimpse turns the videos from your Meta glasses into a highlight video: Gemini
watches them and suggests the best moments, you pick and trim, Glimpse cuts.

You need a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 13 or later.
Nothing else has to be installed.


1. Install it

   Drag Glimpse.app into your Applications folder.


2. Open it the first time

   Glimpse is not notarised by Apple, so macOS blocks it once.

   - Double-click Glimpse. macOS says it cannot check the app. Close the
     message.
   - Open System Settings -> Privacy & Security, scroll down and click
     "Open Anyway". Confirm with your password or Touch ID.

   Or do it in one step: open the Terminal app, paste this line and press
   Return. After that Glimpse opens like any other app.

       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Glimpse.app

   If Glimpse opens but says "Glimpse is damaged", quit it, run that Terminal
   line once and open Glimpse again.


3. Connect Gemini

   Glimpse uses Google's Gemini to find the best moments. Everyone needs their
   own Gemini API key. It is free and takes about two minutes:

   - Go to https://aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with a Google account.
   - Click "Create API key" and copy the key.
   - Paste it into Glimpse on its first screen and click "Connect".

   Without billing the analysis costs nothing, but Google limits it: about
   20 analyses a day with the standard model (one analysis is one video; the
   exact limit is shown in Google AI Studio). When the daily quota is used up,
   Glimpse tells you, and you can wait until the next day or switch to the
   Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite model in Settings, which allows more free analyses.
   For a whole festival you can turn on billing in Google AI Studio. Then a
   video of about 5 minutes costs a few cents, and Glimpse shows the cost
   before the analysis starts.


4. What macOS asks for

   Glimpse keeps your API key encrypted in the macOS Keychain. macOS may ask
   whether Glimpse may use "Glimpse Safe Storage". That is Glimpse's own
   entry. Type your Mac password and click "Always Allow".


5. Updates

   Glimpse does not update itself. When you get a newer Glimpse, quit the app
   and replace Glimpse.app in your Applications folder with the new one. Your
   projects and settings stay. The first time you open the new version, do
   step 2 again, and macOS asks for the Keychain once more (step 4).


Privacy

   Your original videos stay on your Mac. For the analysis, a smaller copy
   (720p) of each video is sent to Google's Gemini and deleted there after
   about 48 hours; removing a video or a project in Glimpse deletes it there
   right away. Without billing, Google may use what you send to improve its
   products, and people at Google may look at it (outside the EU and EEA).
   For footage with other people in it, use an API key with billing turned
   on. Your API key is stored only on your Mac and sent only to Google.
   Nothing is sent to Jan or to anyone else.
